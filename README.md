# SuperTeams Datapack

A Minecraft datapack for team-based arena gameplay. Players choose one of 5 snack-themed teams, each granting a unique permanent buff.

## Teams & Buffs

| Team | Selection Item | Buff |
|------|---------------|------|
| Doritos | Echo Shard | Strength I |
| Cheetos | Phantom Membrane | Speed I |
| Lays | Heart of the Sea | Resistance I |
| Takis | Nautilus Shell | Fire Resistance |
| Pringles | Prismarine Crystals | Haste II |

## How It Works

1. On first join, players receive 5 items (one for each team)
2. Place your chosen team's item in your **offhand** to lock in
3. Other items are cleared and your team buff is applied permanently
4. On death, your team resets and you can pick again

## Installation

1. Download or clone this repo
2. Place the folder in your world's `datapacks/` directory
3. Run `/reload` in-game

## Resource Pack (Recommended)

For custom triangle team icons on the selection items, also install the companion resource pack:
[SuperTeams-Triangle-Icons](https://github.com/TheDyrr/SuperTeams-Triangle-Icons)

## Pack Format

- `pack_format: 61` (Minecraft 1.21.4+)
